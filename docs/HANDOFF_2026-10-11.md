## Rendering style (`render.py`, `docs/FIGURE_STYLE.md`)

The repository's figure style: OVITO + Tachyon with ambient occlusion and soft shadows, light semi-transparent PbX₆
octahedra plus Pb–X sticks, thin uniform grey bonds, ball-and-stick organic cations in element colours (H shown), one
magenta focus atom, an optional gold halo, and no octahedron on a Pb bonded to an adsorbate donor (O, N, S, F).
`display_cutout` rotates the outward normal to +z (it works for a normal along x), replicates 3×3 and keeps a disc
around the focus, so an adsorbate appears once. Views: side, top, perspective. Read `FIGURE_STYLE.md` before making any
structure figure. Don't use ASE's matplotlib plotting for figures: it draws no bonds.

Former limits, all fixed:
1. Incomplete octahedra: they came from a Pb in a neighbouring image bonded to the dropped periodic copy of the focus
   fragment's halide (seen at radius ≥ 11 Å on the 2×2 slab). `Cutout.incomplete` lists such metals and they are drawn
   without an octahedron; a test checks every drawn octahedron has the metal's full halide shell.
2. `render(..., highlight=[indices])`: slightly larger, gold-tinted atoms for a whole adsorbed fragment.
3. `compose.compose_panels`: panel letters, titles, element legend in `render.COLORS`, optional scale bar from a
   declared px/Å (separate process from OVITO).
4. `render.cutout_to_web`: the cutout as JSON (atoms with colour/radius/role, explicit bonds, octahedra) for
   3Dmol/three.js.

## Priorities

1. ~~`classify_final_state` support for oxyanions~~ Done in c4cd19f (`oxidation.oxyanion_state`). Spec was (oxidation handoff item 2): intact IOₙ, I–O cleavage, O transferred to
   Pb, oxyanion lifted out, proton transfer to an oxyanion O. Also add an "I below the outer Pb plane" flag: an iodate
   can sink about 0.5 Å below that plane while its O stay on Pb. Report the orientation of the mean I→O bond against
   the outward normal (0° = O toward vacuum).
2. ~~Pb(IO₃)₂ unit builder~~ Done: `shared_lead_pairs` (cis ~90° / trans ~170–180° in-plane pairs per outer Pb),
   `lead_iodate_poses` (both halides oxidised, second placed with the first present), `lead_iodate_record` (unit declared
   by atom ids), `remove_lead_iodate` (PbI₂ vacancy via `remove_fragments`; refuses if a relaxed iodate is no longer
   intact) and `lead_iodate_extraction_reaction` (requires a declared bulk Species of Z × PbI₂O₆; no default energy).
3. ~~Halide-site classifier~~ Done in fa5fa51: `surface.classify_halides` (in_plane / apical / subsurface / adatom, with
   bridging geometry); `find_sites` sets `Site.halide_class`. Checked on the r2SCAN PbI₂ and FAI 335 slabs: 18 in-plane +
   9 axial per PbI₂ face, 9 apical per FAI face.
4. ~~Orientation presets~~ Done: `oxidation.oxyanion_preset_poses(..., preset="O-down" | "X-down")`. O-down lifts the
   halide by the X–O length and puts one O in the old site, bond straight down; `psis` rotates the rest, psi = 0 puts
   the O–X–O plane perpendicular to the Pb–X–Pb bridge. X-down keeps the halide in its site with O to vacuum; on an
   in-plane PbI₂-face site IO₃⁻ X-down needs `lifts=(0.8, ...)`, otherwise its near-sideways O clash with neighbours.
5. ~~VASP bundle and O₂ correction~~ Done: `vaspprep.oxidation_bundle` writes bare_slab, product and ref_O2
   (triplet: ISPIN = 2, NUPDOWN = 2; box ≥ 15 Å), each relax + static, and refuses unless the static jobs share
   functional, cutoff and PAW variants (`check_matched_settings`; spin and k-mesh may differ). `ledger.ReferenceCorrection`
   is a named per-species shift with a required source, applied only when passed to `Reaction.delta_e`, which then also
   reports the uncorrected value.
6. ~~Render limits 1–4~~ Done (see above).
7. Gaps in `WORKFLOW_AUDIT.md` sections 4 and 7 (missing bare-slab OSZICAR, slab energies that don't match the

## Conventions

- Never guess charges, multiplicities or reference energies.
- Ledger entries must pass `assert_balanced`.
- O₂ is only a reservoir in oxidation reactions.
- Tests use the synthetic `toy_slab`; no proprietary structures in `tests/` or `data/`.
- LF line endings; keep the house figure style.
